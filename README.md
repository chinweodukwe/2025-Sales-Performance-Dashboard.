## 🎯 Project Overview
This project features a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze global sales performance across multiple dimensions. The dashboard provides a high-level executive summary of revenue streams, product popularity, geographical distribution, and monthly sales trends.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, visualization design, and dashboard layout.
* **Excel / CSV:** Source dataset containing sales transactions.

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Top Customer Segment:** The Government and Small Business segments drive the highest sales volume.
* **Primary Market:** The United States represents the largest market share by country, followed closely by Canada.
* **Product Performance:** *Paseo* and *VTT* are the top-performing products, accounting for the highest share of online and in-store sales.
